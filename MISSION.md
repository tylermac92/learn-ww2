# Mission: World War II History

## Why
I'm curious about WWII but start from almost nothing: no books read, only bits from school and films. I want the big picture of why it started, who fought whom, the major turning points and how it ended, so I can follow serious books and documentaries.

## Success looks like
- I can tell the whole story of the war in my own words, in a few minutes, in the right order.
- I can name the sides, their main leaders, and when each major power joined the war.
- I can explain the main causes and the three or four turning points.
- I can pick a book or documentary on a part of the war that interests me and follow it without getting lost.

## Constraints
- Prefers longer, source-driven sessions: point to good books, documentaries and primary sources, then quiz afterwards.
- Wants all theatres: Western Europe, the Eastern Front, the Pacific and Asia, plus causes and politics.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Deep military detail (unit histories, weapons, tactics) before the big picture is solid.
- Counterfactuals and "what if" debates.
