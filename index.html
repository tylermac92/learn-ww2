A course in the Second World War, 1937–1945

WWII Field Course

From “I know bits from school and films” to telling the whole story of the war in your own words, then choosing a serious book or documentary and following it.

Lessons

  1. 01Two Wars, One World War
    Two separate starts, the merge in 1941, two endings in 1945.
  2. 02Why did it start?
    Versailles, the Depression, Hitler, Japanese expansion, appeasement.
  3. 03The Axis at high tide, 1939–1942
  4. 04The turning points: Midway, Stalingrad, El Alamein
  5. 05The road to victory, 1943–1945
