Reference · the course’s canonical terms

Glossary

The words this course uses, and how it uses them. A term is added once you have used it correctly.

Sides

Axis
The alliance of Germany, Italy and Japan, with smaller partners. Avoid: calling the USSR of 1939–41 “Axis”. It was Germany’s partner by pact, but never joined the Axis.
Allies
The coalition that fought the Axis, led by the “Big Three” (Britain, the USSR from 1941, the US from 1941), together with China and France.

Places and fronts

Theatre
A major region of the war treated as one area of fighting, such as the European theatre or the Pacific theatre.
Eastern Front
The war between Germany (with its allies) and the USSR, from June 1941 to May 1945. Avoid: using it for the war against Japan. That is the Pacific theatre.

Events

Operation Barbarossa
Germany’s invasion of the USSR, launched on 22 June 1941. It turned the USSR from Germany’s partner into an Ally.
