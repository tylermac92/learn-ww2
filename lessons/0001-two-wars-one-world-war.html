Lesson 01 · The big picture · about 25 minutes with the reading

Two Wars, One World War

If you remember one idea from this course, make it this one. The Second World War began as two separate wars on opposite sides of the world. They merged into a single global war in December 1941. Once you can place an event in one war or the other, every book and documentary gets easier to follow.

The war in Asia, from 1937

Japan moved first. In September 1931 Japanese troops seized the Manchurian city of Mukden, then invaded the rest of Manchuria, in northeast China.Britannica, “Mukden Incident.” That was a limited conflict. Full-scale war came on 7 July 1937, when a clash between Chinese and Japanese troops at the Marco Polo Bridge near Beijing set off the Sino-Japanese War. That war ran until Japan’s surrender in 1945 and in 1941 became part of the Pacific war.Britannica, “Marco Polo Bridge Incident.”

This is why many historians, especially in China, date the start of the Second World War to 1937, not 1939.

The war in Europe, from 1939

On 1 September 1939 Germany invaded Poland. Britain and France declared war on Germany two days later, on 3 September.Britannica, “World War II” summary; Britannica facts page, which dates the war from 3 September 1939.

Now the twist that films tend to skip. At this stage the Soviet Union was not on the Allied side. A week before the invasion, on 23 August 1939, Germany and the USSR signed a pact. It included a secret protocol to divide Poland between them, and Soviet troops invaded Poland from the east on 17 September.USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia, “German-Soviet Pact.”

1941: the year the wars merged

Two attacks, six months apart, pulled the last great powers in.

Germany turns east. On 22 June 1941 Germany invaded the Soviet Union. It was codenamed Operation Barbarossa and was the largest German military operation of the war.USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia, “Invasion of the Soviet Union, June 1941.” Germany’s former partner became Britain’s ally overnight.

Japan strikes the United States. On 7 December 1941 Japan attacked the US fleet at Pearl Harbor, and Congress declared war on Japan the next day. On 11 December Germany declared war on the United States. Historians Brendan Simms and Charles Laderman argue that this declaration mattered even more than Pearl Harbor itself, because it “created a new global strategic reality.”Quoted in Sean Scanlon, “Hitler’s Declaration of War on the United States,” The National WWII Museum, 2024. From that day there was one war, with the same two sides in Europe and the Pacific.

1945: two endings

The merged war ended twice. Germany surrendered first: the Allies accepted its official surrender on 8 May 1945, Victory in Europe Day. Japan announced its surrender on 14 August (US time) and signed the formal surrender aboard the USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay on 2 September 1945.The National WWII Museum, “V-J Day” fact sheet. Celebrations on V-E Day were muted for many people, because the war in the Pacific went on.

Your reading

“Hitler’s Declaration of War on the United States” by Sean Scanlon, a historian at The National WWII Museum (2024). It takes about ten minutes.

It covers the exact moment the two wars became one, and why Hitler chose it. As you read, look for one answer: why would Germany declare war on the US when it didn’t have to? Bring your answer to our next session.

Practice: retrieval

Answer from memory. If you aren’t sure, guess, then read the explanation: a wrong guess you correct is remembered better than a page you reread.

Before Germany invaded Poland in 1939, where was a major war already under way?

Japan and China had been at full-scale war since the Marco Polo Bridge clash in July 1937.

Which event is usually taken as the start of the war in Europe?

Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939, and Britain and France declared war on 3 September.

In September 1939, what was the Soviet Union to Germany?

The pact of 23 August 1939 had a secret protocol to divide Poland. Soviet troops invaded from the east on 17 September.

Which two moves in 1941 turned two regional wars into one world war?

Operation Barbarossa (22 June) brought the USSR in on the Allied side. Pearl Harbor (7 December) brought in the United States.

Pearl Harbor put the US at war with Japan. Why was it also at war with Germany within days?

Germany declared war on the United States on 11 December 1941. The American invasion of France came in 1944.

Of Germany and Japan, which surrendered first, and when?

Germany surrendered in May 1945 (V-E Day, 8 May). Japan announced its surrender in August and signed on 2 September 1945.

Why do many historians, especially in China, date the war’s start to 1937?

Full-scale war between Japan and China began in July 1937 and merged into the world war in 1941. Hitler took power in 1933, and the US entered in 1941.

Practice: tell the story

This is the real test, and it’s what your mission asks for. Without scrolling up, write the whole war in four sentences: one each for 1937, 1939, 1941 and 1945.

Compare with a model answer

In 1937 Japan, already holding Manchuria, began a full-scale war with China. In 1939 Germany invaded Poland, Britain and France declared war, and the Soviet Union, Germany’s partner for now, took eastern Poland. In 1941 Germany invaded the Soviet Union and Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, and Germany then declared war on the US, so the two wars became one. In 1945 Germany surrendered in May and Japan in August, signing on 2 September.

Check yours for the four dates, the two separate starts, the merge, and the two endings. Wording doesn’t matter.
